============================================================
DISEASE: LEAF VARIEGATION on Cotton (Kapas)
Region: Punjab & Sindh, Pakistan
============================================================

--- DISEASE IDENTIFICATION ---
Type          : Physiological/Nutritional Disorder (Abiotic) — NOT infectious
Local Name    : Patta Ranga Badalna, Rang Birangi Pattiyan
Affected Stage: All growth stages; most visible during vegetative and boll development

Symptoms:
- Irregular yellow, cream, or white patches/streaks on green leaves (mosaic-like)
- Leaf blade shows two or more distinct colors (hence "variegation")
- Veins may remain green while interveinal areas turn yellow or pale
- In severe cases, entire leaves may bleach/whiten
- Can be confused with virus symptoms but is typically non-spreading (abiotic cause)
- Stunted growth when nutrient deficiency is severe
- Commonly appears on young/new growth first

Primary Causes of Leaf Variegation in Pakistan Cotton:
1. Magnesium (Mg) Deficiency — most common in sandy soils of Sindh
2. Iron (Fe) Deficiency — Lime-induced chlorosis in calcareous soils (common in Punjab)
3. Manganese (Mn) Deficiency
4. Zinc (Zn) Deficiency — widespread in Pakistan cotton soils
5. Sulfur (S) Deficiency
6. Virus infection (Cotton Leaf Curl Virus) — if accompanied by leaf curling/thickening
7. Excessive soil alkalinity (pH > 8.0) blocking micronutrient uptake

Note: Pakistan soils (particularly Punjab and Sindh) are highly alkaline (pH 7.5-8.5),
which severely reduces availability of Fe, Mn, Zn causing leaf variegation.

============================================================
RECOMMENDED SPRAYS/TREATMENTS — PAKISTAN LOCAL MARKET
============================================================

FOR MAGNESIUM DEFICIENCY VARIEGATION:
--------------------------------------
1. Magnesium Sulphate (Epsom Salt) Foliar Spray
   Formulation : Magnesium Sulphate (MgSO4) — available in all agri shops
   Local Name  : Khat Magnesium / Epsom Salt
   Dose        : 20-25 g per 10 liters of water (2-2.5 kg per acre in 100L water)
   Application : Spray 2-3 times at 10-day intervals
   Availability: Widely available across Punjab & Sindh; brands include Engro Zarkhez Mg, local chemical shops
   Notes       : Also apply 25 kg/hectare soil application before planting in Mg-deficient areas

FOR IRON/ZINC/MANGANESE DEFICIENCY VARIEGATION:
------------------------------------------------
2. Chelated Micronutrient Foliar Spray (Multi-Micronutrient)
   Products Available in Pakistan:
   - Zarkhez Micronutrient Fertilizer (Engro Fertilizers Pakistan) — widely trusted in Punjab/Sindh
   - Multi-K (Haifa / local distributors)
   - Nutribuild / Nutrifeed (various local companies)
   - Librel / Sequestrene (chelated iron — for iron-specific variegation)
   Dose        : Follow label; typically 200-300 g per acre foliar spray
   Application : 2-3 sprays at 10-day intervals during active growth

3. Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO4) for Iron Deficiency
   Dose        : 2-3 g per liter of water (200-300 g per acre in 100L water)
   Important   : Add Citric Acid (1 g per liter) to prevent precipitation in alkaline water
   Notes       : Avoid mixing with phosphate fertilizers (precipitates); apply separately

4. Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4) for Zinc Deficiency Variegation
   Dose        : 2.5 g per liter of water (250 g per acre in 100L water)
   Local Brands: Engro Zn Sulphate, Fauji Zinc Sulphate, Ittehad Chemicals
   Application : Soil application @ 10-15 kg per acre if deficiency severe
   Notes       : Zinc deficiency is extremely common in Pakistani soils — standard recommendation for cotton in Punjab

FOR SULFUR DEFICIENCY VARIEGATION:
-----------------------------------
5. Potassium Sulphate (SOP) or Ammonium Sulphate foliar
   Dose        : Ammonium Sulphate 1% solution spray (10 g per liter)
   Notes       : Particularly important in irrigated Sindh soils

COMBINED APPROACH (Best Practice for Mixed Deficiency):
-------------------------------------------------------
6. Urea + MgSO4 Combination Spray
   Dose        : Urea 1% (10 g/L) + Magnesium Sulphate 0.5% (5 g/L)
   Application : 2-3 foliar sprays at 10-15 day intervals
   Notes       : This combination is officially recommended by Punjab Agriculture Extension for cotton leaf variegation

7. Humic Acid + Micronutrient Package
   Products    : Humate Gold (Sayban International), Agri-Plus (local brands), Humic Pro
   Dose        : 250-500 ml per acre
   Notes       : Humic acid improves nutrient availability in alkaline soils; spray with micronutrients

============================================================
WEATHER-BASED SPRAY TIMING GUIDE (FOR RAG AGENT)
============================================================

CONDITION 1 — HOT SUMMER (Temp: 38-45°C, Humidity: < 40%)
  → Micronutrient uptake severely impaired; variegation worsens
  → SPRAY: Chelated Fe + Zn foliar spray (chelated forms more stable in heat)
  → Spray time: ONLY early morning (5:00-6:30 AM) or evening (7-8 PM)
  → DO NOT spray foliar nutrients in midday heat — leaf burn risk (scorching)
  → Increase irrigation frequency; drought stress worsens nutrient deficiency
  → Spray every 10 days until recovery; 3 sprays minimum

CONDITION 2 — WARM MODERATE (Temp: 28-35°C, Humidity: 50-65%)
  → Best conditions for foliar nutrient uptake
  → SPRAY: Urea 1% + MgSO4 0.5% combination
  → Morning spray (6-8 AM) — stomata open, absorption best
  → Apply 3 sprays at 10-day intervals for full recovery
  → Add sticker to improve foliar adhesion

CONDITION 3 — AFTER RAIN / HIGH HUMIDITY (Humidity > 70%)
  → Good spray window — stomata wide open, absorption excellent
  → Spray within 4-6 hours after rain (when leaves are dry but humidity still high)
  → MgSO4 + Zinc foliar spray most effective in these conditions
  → Avoid if rain expected within 3 hours (spray washes off)

CONDITION 4 — WATERLOGGED / FLOODED SOIL
  → Variegation may worsen due to root oxygen stress blocking nutrient uptake
  → Drain field FIRST — do not spray nutrients on waterlogged plants (ineffective)
  → After drainage, apply foliar spray within 48 hours
  → Also add potassium (SOP 1% solution) to restore root function

CONDITION 5 — CLOUDY / OVERCAST (No direct sunlight)
  → Excellent for foliar spray application any time of day
  → UV exposure is low; nutrients remain active longer on leaf surface
  → Best window for iron chelate sprays (Fe chelates break down in UV)

CONDITION 6 — HIGH ALKALINE SOIL CONDITIONS (pH > 8.0 — common in Punjab canal areas)
  → Use EDDHA-chelated iron (more stable in high pH) instead of FeSO4
  → Apply soil acidifiers (Sulfur @ 100-200 kg/ha) to lower pH over time
  → Foliar sprays are critical because soil application is ineffective above pH 7.5

============================================================
SOIL TEST RECOMMENDATION
============================================================
- Always do soil testing before assuming deficiency type
- Punjab Agriculture Department offers free soil testing at district labs
- Sindh Agriculture Department (SADO offices) also provide soil testing
- Key soil parameters: pH, EC, Organic Matter, NPK, Zn, Fe, Mn, B
- Target soil pH for cotton: 6.5-7.5 (adjust with gypsum or sulfur if needed)

============================================================
INTEGRATED MANAGEMENT
============================================================
- Apply gypsum (CaSO4) @ 500-1000 kg/acre to improve Ca+Mg availability and correct Mg deficiency
- Use organic matter / FYM (Farm Yard Manure) @ 5-10 t/ha to improve nutrient release
- Avoid excessive P fertilizer — competes with Zn and Fe uptake
- Use balanced NPK fertilizer program recommended by Punjab/Sindh Agriculture Dept
